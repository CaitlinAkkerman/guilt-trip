Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Guilt Trip is a task tracker that makes ignoring your tasks uncomfortable. This page explains, in plain language, what information it handles and what you can do about it.

Who runs this

Guilt Trip is run by Caitlin Akkerman. You can reach me at akkermancaitlin@gmail.com.

What the app stores

  • On your device, always: your tasks (names, due dates, completion and reschedule details) and your settings, saved in your browser. If you never sign in, this is the only place your tasks live, and I never see them.
  • In your account, only if you sign in: your email address and a copy of your tasks, so they can sync across your devices.
  • Standard web data: like any website, the services that deliver this site may see technical information your browser sends, such as your IP address.

What I don’t do

No ads. No selling or renting your information. No analytics or tracking tools are built into the app.

Why the information is used

Only to run the app: to sign you in with an emailed link, to keep your tasks in sync, and to show them back to you.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase provides the database, sign-in, and the email that delivers your sign-in link.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the files that make up the site.

These services process information on my behalf and have their own privacy policies. I don’t share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long it’s kept, and deleting it

Your account and synced tasks are kept until you delete them. When signed in, use the Delete my account button in the app to permanently remove your account and every task synced to it. You can also use Export tasks first if you want a copy.

Note: when you use “Clear history,” those tasks are hidden from you and marked as deleted, but they remain in the database until you delete your whole account.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and the database is set up so each account can only read and change its own tasks. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep the next point in mind.

Please don’t put sensitive details in tasks

Avoid entering passwords, financial account numbers, health information, or confidential client or customer details in task names.

Children

Guilt Trip is not intended for children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect information from them.
